# Changelog

## Unreleased

- One disc per kind of sound: Rain, Forest, Birds, Creek, Ocean, Coffee shop, White noise, Pink noise, Brown noise and Box breathing, grouped as Nature, Places, Noise and Mindfulness. The extra rain, forest and ocean variations are gone.
- New discs: Creek and Coffee shop (CC0 recordings), the three noises (generated), and Box breathing, a 4-4-4-4 breathing guide that loops seamlessly. Each has its own disc art.
- The white highlight streak on every disc is gone (san, 2026-10-24).
- Files an hour or longer show their elapsed and total times on one line (they used to wrap), and the scrub line keeps its length while the time ticks. Checked on iPhone SE with titles in Japanese, very long titles and a 10-hour file.

## 2026-10-18

- A swipeable CD carousel replaces the sound shelf and tabs. Swipe anywhere, including on the name, to move one disc; tap the centered disc to play or pause.
- The main screen is one centered group: the disc sits inside a volume ring you drag, with − / play / + below it. The ring shows the level, so the percentage readout and the Ready / Looping / Paused labels are gone.
- One file at a time: every disc loops on its own, and each file you add is its own disc. Loop one, Play all, Shuffle and the single Your mix disc are gone.
- Your files (modes, Add files, list) moved into its own sheet, opened from a button at the top right.
- Your files show their embedded cover art on their disc, with a glow picked from the cover, and each file resumes where you left it. A slim scrub line under the title jumps anywhere in the file.
- FLAC support, including titles, artists, albums and covers.
- New disc art for the built-in sounds, one colour story per sound, and each disc glows in its own colour.
- New app icon: two glowing translucent records, cyan and magenta overlapping into violet, on midnight navy.
- An error line appears if audio fails to start.

## 2026-10-17

- Renamed the app to Duet.
- One screen with a CD library: discs slide out of their case and spin while playing, with a drag-to-set volume bar.
- Built-in looping sounds, grouped into Noise, Rain, Forest and Ocean.
- Your files are remembered across launches. Folder import is gone.
- Fixed play doing nothing on first launch, no resume after interruptions, and swipe-to-delete not working.

## 2026-05-28

- First version: plays audio under YouTube or another audio app instead of stopping it.
- Plays files from a folder or picked one by one (for Google Drive and other providers that don't allow folder selection), with shuffle, previous/next, play/pause, progress and volume.
- Shows each file's title, artist, album and artwork, with an accent colour taken from the artwork.
